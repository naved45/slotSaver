# 1. PRD — SlotSaver

**Product promise:** "Get more bookings. Less no-shows."
**Tagline:** Smart Appointment Scheduling for Your Business.

## Problem
Small service businesses (dental clinics, salons, physiotherapy, wellness studios, consultants) lose revenue to cancelled and unfilled appointment slots and to no-shows. Most use paper, spreadsheets, or generic calendars with no way to refill a cancelled slot quickly.

## Core differentiator
When an appointment is cancelled, SlotSaver finds eligible waitlisted customers and safely offers the freed time. Only one customer can hold an offer for a slot at a time, and only the first valid acceptance reserves it.

## Target users
- **Owner** — configures the business and team, sees analytics.
- **Admin/Manager** — runs daily operations.
- **Staff** — sees their own assigned appointments.
- **Customer (guest)** — books, reschedules, cancels, and accepts slot offers from email links.

## Goals (MVP)
1. Owner can sign up, verify email, and configure timezone, hours, services, staff, and booking rules.
2. Customers can book, reschedule, and cancel through public pages.
3. Double booking is prevented, including simultaneous requests.
4. Cancelled slots generate ranked waitlist offers; expiry and decline advance to the next candidate.
5. Reminders and confirmations are queued durably and tracked with honest delivery status.
6. Dashboard and analytics compute metrics from real database records.
7. Every important action is written to an audit log.

## Non-goals
- No medical records or clinical notes. Scheduling only.
- No payment collection until a payment provider is configured (pricing page is informational).
- No native mobile apps; responsive web only.
- No claims of compliance certification (GDPR/HIPAA etc.) without an actual assessment.

## Key assumptions
- Currency defaults to INR for demo data; each service stores its own currency.
- Pricing (Free, Starter ₹799/mo, Growth ₹2,499/mo) is **proposed pricing to validate**, not proven market pricing.
- Demo data is fictional.
- One organization per owner account at launch; schema supports multi-org membership.

## Success metrics (defined in DESIGN/ANALYTICS docs)
- Cancellation rate, no-show rate, recovered bookings, estimated recovered revenue (confirmed appointments attributed to the waitlist workflow × service price), offer acceptance rate, time from cancellation to rebooking.

## Open questions (resolved by assumption, to revisit)
- Email provider: SMTP via Nodemailer; dev fallback logs previews.
- Whether customers may self-reschedule without an account: yes, via expiring signed links.
